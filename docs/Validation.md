## Initial Data Exploration

After the data was cleaned and loaded into MySQL, SQL queries were used to explore the dataset and understand the business context before building dashboards and KPIs.

The analysis focused on several key areas:

### Sales Analysis

Sales-related KPIs were analyzed to understand overall business performance, including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Average order value
* Monthly Revenue Trend

### Customer Analysis

Customer data was explored to understand customer behavior and purchasing patterns, including:

* Repeat Customers
* Top Customer States
* Average Review Score by State

### Delivery Analysis

Order and delivery data were analyzed to evaluate logistics performance, including:

* Average Delivery Days
* Late Delivery Percentage
* States with Highest Delivery Delays
* Relationship Between Delays & Reviews

### Product Analysis

Product-related metrics were examined to identify top-performing and low-performing categories, including:

* Top Product Categories
* Most Ordered Products

### Seller Analysis

Seller performance was analyzed to understand marketplace activity, including:

* Top Sellers by Revenue
* Seller Review Performance

### Outcome

This exploratory analysis helped build a better understanding of the dataset, business processes, and relationships between different entities. The insights gained during this stage were used to identify important KPIs and guide the design of the dashboard and reporting layer.
